How to Choose Online Quran Classes for Kids

Every parent wants the same thing: a teacher their child loves, lessons that actually stick, and honest updates on progress. Before you enrol anywhere, check these six things — they are exactly what we built Noor Ul Huda Online Quran Academy around.

Six things to check before you enrol your child

1

One-on-One Attention

In a 1-on-1 class your child is never lost in a crowd. The teacher corrects every letter and recitation on the spot, so progress is steady week after week.

2

Qualified Male & Female Teachers

Ask who will teach your child. We have both male and female Quran teachers, so families can choose the teacher their child is most comfortable with.

3

A Free Trial Class First

Never pay before you have seen a real class. A free trial lets your child meet the teacher, try the lesson style, and lets you judge the quality yourself.

4

Timing That Fits Your Family

Online classes should work around school, homework and family time — not the other way round. Choose days and times that suit your child, with easy rescheduling. We teach families in the USA, UK, Canada and Australia.

5

Visible Progress Reports

You should always know what your child learned this week. We send parents a weekly WhatsApp progress report, so nothing about your child’s learning stays hidden.

6

Safe Learning at Home

Classes happen at home, on your own device, with you nearby. There is no travel, no waiting outside a classroom, and full peace of mind for parents.
